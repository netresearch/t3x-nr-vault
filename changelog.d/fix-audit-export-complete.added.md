- `vault:audit --verify-export=FILE` re-verifies a JSON export from its own rows: exact row shape, every row against its entry hash, the links between rows, epoch decreases and the declared row count. It reports the export as the whole chain only when it can confirm that against the installation's stored chain and the external anchor. Rows at HMAC epoch 1 and above need the master key that was in effect when the export was taken.
