- The decommissioning runbook stops every process that writes audit rows before the final evidence export, keeps them stopped until the master key is destroyed, and orders the evidence steps as verify, export, publish the final anchor, verify the export. A new mandatory check confirms that the final anchor's sequence and chain tip match the end of the export and the verified chain length, and the key destruction step re-checks that no row was written after the evidence closed. Before, a row written between the steps could end up outside the anchored, verified evidence.
